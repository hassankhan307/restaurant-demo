The philosophy

Simple ingredients, serious fire.

Every dish at Ember & Olive begins with live flame. We built the kitchen around an open hearth, not a range — it changes how we buy, how we prep, and how a plate finally tastes.

The menu moves with the produce worth celebrating that week, and stays honest to a Mediterranean table: good oil, citrus, char, and restraint.
